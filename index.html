First things, first day

A tiny cheat-sheet for the moment we land at Haneda so we hit the ground running.

💴Cash first

Japan still loves cash. Withdraw yen at a Haneda 7-Bank / Japan Post ATM (Revolut works well). Carry coins for shrines & vending machines.

🚆Welcome Suica

Grab a Welcome Suica at the airport and load ¥3,000–5,000. Tap onto every train, bus and convenience-store till.

📕Goshuincho

Buy a goshuincho (stamp book) at the first big shrine — Meiji Jingu or Senso-ji — then collect a goshuin at every temple after.
